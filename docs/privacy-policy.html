Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

RiseUp is designed to be local-first. Your personal growth data is stored on your device, and most features work without creating an account.

1. Information you provide

RiseUp stores information you choose to enter, such as your display name, to-dos and sub-tasks, habit-tracker entries, check-ins, mood and energy entries, reflections, journal or practice entries, preferences, and AI-chat messages.

2. How your information is stored

Your RiseUp data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database. RiseUp does not operate its own account system or cloud database for this information, and it does not sell your personal information.

Because your data is stored on your device, clearing RiseUp's app data or uninstalling the app permanently removes the locally stored information. Please keep this in mind before clearing data or removing the app.

3. Optional AI features and your Gemini API key

RiseUp offers optional AI-supported chat and weekly insights. AI features are disabled unless you choose to add your own Google Gemini API key. RiseUp does not provide, collect, or store a developer API key for these features.

Your Gemini API key is stored only on your device using the platform's encrypted secure storage. On Android, this uses encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore; on iOS, it uses the Keychain. RiseUp does not send your key to a RiseUp server, does not keep a copy of it, and does not share it with anyone other than Google Gemini to authenticate your request.

When you use an AI feature, RiseUp sends the content needed to provide that feature directly to Google's Gemini API using your key. Depending on the feature, this may include your message, recent chat history, display name, active to-do titles and sub-task counts, mood or energy context, or an aggregated completed-task count.

You can remove your Gemini API key at any time in RiseUp Settings; this deletes it from the app's secure local storage. Clearing RiseUp's app data or uninstalling the app also removes the locally stored key. Do not enter sensitive personal, health, financial, or confidential information into AI prompts. Google's handling of information sent to Gemini is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, RiseUp schedules reminders locally on your device for to-dos, habits, and other enabled app features. Notification content can include the title of a to-do so that the reminder is useful. You can manage notification permission in your device settings and within RiseUp where available.

5. Sharing and disclosure

RiseUp does not sell or rent your personal information. Apart from the optional Gemini requests described above, RiseUp does not transfer the personal information you enter to a third-party cloud service as part of its normal operation.

6. Data retention and your choices

You control your locally stored data. You may edit or delete individual entries in the app when those controls are available. You can also remove all locally stored RiseUp data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. AI-chat conversations can be deleted from the chat feature.

7. Children's privacy

RiseUp is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can help address the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as RiseUp changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when we make material changes.
